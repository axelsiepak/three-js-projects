# Rotating Cube

A rotating purple cube, displayed fullscreen, that you can view from any angle with your mouse.

<p align="center">
	<img src="demo.gif" alt="The cube rotating while the camera orbits around it">
</p>

## Controls

| Action | Mouse | Touch | Keyboard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Orbit around the cube | Left-click + drag | Drag with one finger | Arrow keys |
| Zoom in or out | Scroll wheel | Pinch with two fingers | – |
| Pan the view | Right-click + drag | Drag with two fingers | – |
